01 / TRANSPORTATION OPERATIONS
PRACTICAL PROJECT

On-Time Delivery & Time-Saving Management

A transportation process-improvement case focused on improving delivery punctuality by prioritizing dispatches, controlling exceptions and reducing avoidable time loss.

01Dispatch priority workflow
02Exception checkpoints
03Time-loss controls
KPIOn-time delivery tracking

Approach

  • Prioritize deliveries by scheduled time, urgency and operational constraints.
  • Track dispatch-to-delivery milestones and flag exceptions early.
  • Identify recurring causes of delay such as late dispatch, documentation gaps and route issues.
  • Use daily exception reviews to assign follow-up and reduce avoidable waiting time.

Measures

On-time delivery rate · Dispatch turnaround time · Delay frequency · Exception closure time · Waiting time · Daily delivery completion
